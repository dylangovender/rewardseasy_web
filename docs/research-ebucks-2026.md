## Key mechanics  [HIGH]
- Level reviewed monthly (~8th); no point carry-over. eBucks paid on the 15th.
- 10 qualifying debit transactions required to earn the income-deposit points; miss them → capped at Level 3.
- 70+ clients exempt from Virtual Card requirement.
- Supa Quick removed as earn partner from 1 July 2026.
