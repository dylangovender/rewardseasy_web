## Recent comings and goings

- **Woolworths joined eBucks** on 1 October 2026 (FNB Private Clients, Private Wealth and RMB Private Bank only).
- **SPAR left eBucks** on 30 September 2026, for both earning and spending.
- **Supa Quick and iStore left eBucks** on 1 July 2026.
- **Clicks joined UCount** in 2026 (up to 30% back). **Dis-Chem left UCount** on 28 February 2026.
- **Checkers and Shoprite moved from eBucks to UCount** in April 2025.
