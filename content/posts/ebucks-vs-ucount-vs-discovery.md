The three big South African bank rewards programmes, side by side. eBucks figures are 2026/27; UCount and Discovery are current (2026).

## At a glance

| | eBucks (FNB) | UCount (Standard Bank) | Discovery Miles |
|---|---|---|---|
| **How status works** | FNB segment × reward level 1–5 | Tiering Points → Tier 1–5 (top = 875+) | Vitality Money status (Blue→Diamond) from 6 behaviours |
| **Points value** | R1 = eB10 (eB1 = 10c) | 10 points = R1 | 10 Miles = R1 |
| **Monthly fee** | Included with account | R25/month | Included with account |
| **Top groceries** | PnP asap! up to **30%** | Checkers/Shoprite up to **30%** (40% Sixty60) | Checkers/Woolworths up to **25%** (up to 75% with bank boost) |
| **Fuel partner** | **Engen**, up to R8/ℓ | **Caltex/Astron**, up to R5/ℓ (R10 stacked) | **bp & select Shell**, up to 20% back |
| **Complexity** | Medium–high | Medium | High |

## Where each one rewards you

| Category | eBucks | UCount | Discovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Groceries | Pick n Pay, SPAR, **Checkers** | **Checkers**/Shoprite, Makro | **Checkers**, Woolworths |
| Fuel | Engen | Caltex / Astron | bp, select Shell |
| Pharmacy/health | **Clicks** | — | **Clicks**, Dis-Chem |
| Clothing | PnP Clothing | (CYOR fashion) | ALDO, Cape Union Mart, Superbalist |
| Devices/tech | eBucks Shop (up to 100%) | Game, Makro | Miles+ partners |

**The overlap is small and telling:** **Checkers** is the only grocer all three reward, **Clicks** is shared by eBucks and Discovery, and **fuel is completely split** — Engen, Caltex and bp/Shell each belong to one programme. So your bank effectively picks your forecourt. *(A visual Venn of these overlaps is coming.)*

## Quick verdicts

**eBucks** — the most generous at the top end (R8/ℓ fuel, 30% groceries, 100% on devices) if you're an FNB Premier+ client who consolidates banking, insurance and spend with FNB. Layered, but this site exists to make it simple.

**UCount** — the cleanest model: one points wallet, one fixed value (10 = R1), one tier ladder. Costs R25/month and tops out a little lower than eBucks, but easier to follow. Strong if you shop Checkers/Shoprite and fuel at Caltex.

**Discovery Miles** — potentially the highest rewards (up to 75% on HealthyFood, 75% off flights) but by far the most complex: your return depends on Vitality Money status, card tier, spend band and whether you also have Vitality Health. Rewards the all-in Discovery household.
