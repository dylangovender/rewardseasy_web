Everything on this site has one goal: get you to **eBucks reward Level 5**, where the earn rates are 5–10× higher than Level 1. Here's the whole system in one screen.

These rules take effect **1 July 2026** and run to 30 June 2027.

## Two things, not one

eBucks has two separate systems people constantly confuse:

1. **Reward-level points** — how you *qualify* for a level (1–5). Earned by banking well: deposits, savings, insurance, digital engagement.
2. **Earn rates** — what you *get back* once you're on that level. Higher level = bigger % back at partners.

This page is about reaching Level 5. The [monthly checklist]({{< ref "monthly-reminders" >}}) and [once-offs]({{< ref "once-offs" >}}) tell you exactly how.

## Points needed for Level 5

| Account | Level 5 |
|---|---|
| FNB Premier | **9,500** points |
| FNB Private Clients | **11,000** points |
| FNB Private Wealth | **13,000** points |
| RMB Private Bank | **13,000** points |

*Good news: these dropped for 2026/27, so Level 5 is easier than last year.* Full level bands are on the [limits & targets]({{< ref "limits-and-targets" >}}) page.

## How the monthly cycle works

- Points are collected **per calendar month** and **don't carry over**.
- Your level is **reviewed around the 8th**, based on last month's behaviour.
- eBucks are **paid on the 15th**.
- So: act early in the month, and check **Track my rewards** before month-end.

{{< notice warning "The Level 3 trap" >}}
To earn the big income-deposit points you must make your monthly deposit **and** at least **10 qualifying debit transactions**. Miss the 10 transactions and you're **capped at Level 3** — no matter how many other points you earn.
{{< /notice >}}

## The one habit that powers everything: the Virtual Card

Pay with your **FNB Virtual Card** for at least 80% of your spend. As of 2026/27 this no longer earns levelling points — **but you still need it** to earn the high partner rates (up to 30% groceries, R8/ℓ fuel) and to unlock Smart Spend. Clients aged 70+ are exempt from the Virtual Card requirement.
