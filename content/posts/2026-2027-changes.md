The new rules took effect **1 July 2026**. If your eBucks routine is a year or two old, these are the things that will trip you up.

## Level 5 got easier

Thresholds dropped across the board:

| Account | Old Level 5 | New Level 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Premier | 10,000 | **9,500** |
| Private Clients | 11,500 | **11,000** |
| Private Wealth | 13,500 | **13,000** |
| RMB Private Bank | 13,500 | **13,000** |

## Biggest change: Virtual Card points are gone

Using your Virtual Card for 80%+ of spend used to be worth up to **1,500 levelling points**. For 2026/27 it's **zero**. You still *must* use the Virtual Card to earn partner rates and Smart Spend — it just no longer counts towards your level. If your strategy was "swipe the VCC for points," that strategy is dead.

## Smaller changes

- **Digital engagement back to 500** (after a cut to 250) — but now needs more: open *both* Track my rewards *and* the new **Earn more** tab; nav» Money needs 2+ tools.
- **Smart Spend reduced** — roughly 15% less mid-tier, up to ~40% less at the Level 5 maximum.
- **New points** for Islamic banking, scheduled savings transfers, and additional insurance/retirement categories.
- **Partners:** Supa Quick removed (1 July 2026). New spend partners include Koodoo and Golfers Club; eBucks Travel now up to **80% off** flights.
- **Admin:** eBucks now paid on the **15th**; your level is calculated around the **8th**.

## Mid-year update (October 2026)

- **SPAR left eBucks** on 30 September 2026, for both earning and spending.
- **Woolworths joined** on 1 October 2026 for FNB Private Clients, Private Wealth and RMB Private Bank (not Premier): up to R600 or R1,000 back a month. Rates on [limits & targets]({{< ref "limits-and-targets" >}}).
- See who partners with whom on the [partner overlap Venn]({{< ref "partner-overlap" >}}).

## What to drop from older guides

- The "use your Virtual Card for points" advice — no longer true.
- The old Level 5 thresholds (11,000 / 12,500 / 14,500 era).
- Earning points by logging into Udemy / Extramarks / The Entertainer — those are now perks, not points.
